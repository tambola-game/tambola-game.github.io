# Host cheat sheet: Tambola, GIVE 2026
**Thu 29 Oct 2026 · 12:00–1:00 PM IST · Microsoft Teams**

## Before you start (11:45–11:55)
- Open `host.html`, click **New game**, and keep it on your own screen. You are **not** sharing it, so players only hear the numbers.
- Turn **Read aloud** off. The voice plays on your speakers and won't reach the Teams call. You say each number yourself.
- Don't rely on auto-call. Use **Call next** (Space) so the pace matches your speaking.
- Ask a co-host to type each called number into the chat, if possible.
- Open the tickets page in the same browser if you want winners' names looked up.
- Keep the chat visible. Keep the tickets zip or the registry export handy as a backup.

## Opening (about 2 min, 12:00)
> "Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to **Tambola 2026**, part of our **GIVE 2026** event! 🎉 Thank you for joining and for the generous donations that made this happen.
>
> Quick check: can you hear me clearly? Type **'Hi'** in the chat if so. Great, I see a lot of 'Hi's!
>
> Please have your printed tickets ready, plus a pen or pencil. If you didn't print, keep the ticket open on screen. I'll call about 90 numbers, and we have prizes to win. Let's have fun, and may the luckiest ticket win!"

## Rules to explain (about 3 min)
> "Here's how it works."

1. **Numbers 1 to 90** are called at random. Each number is called only once, so no repeats.
2. **Mark each number** on your ticket as I call it. I'll say each number clearly, and I'll post the list of called numbers in the chat every so often, so you can catch up if you miss one.
3. **Prizes.** The first player to complete each one wins that prize:

| Prize | What to complete |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Early Five** | Any 5 numbers on your ticket |
| 🌡️ **Lucky Seven** | Any 7 numbers on your ticket |
| ⬇️ **Top Line** | All 5 numbers in row 1 |
| ↔️ **Middle Line** | All 5 numbers in row 2 |
| ⬆️ **Bottom Line** | All 5 numbers in row 3 |
| ⛹ **Four Corners** | First and last number of the top row, and of the bottom row |
| ✨ **Star / Centre** | The middle number of row 2, which is the 3rd of its 5 numbers. Count the numbers, not the columns. |
| 👑 **Full House** | All 15 numbers on your ticket |
| 🥇 **Second Full House** | The next ticket to complete all 15, after the first. It must be a different ticket. |

4. **Claiming a win:**
   - Type **"Bingo"** in the chat, followed by the prize name (for example "Bingo – Top Line").
   - When I call on you, show a **snapshot of your ticket**.
   - I'll check the numbers against what has been called.
5. **Ties:** whoever typed "Bingo" first in the chat wins.
6. **Multiple wins:** one person can win several prizes, but the two Full Houses need different tickets.
7. **Be honest.** A wrong claim is a "bogey". We'll laugh and carry on. Don't mark a number until it has been called.

> "Any questions? Great, let's play. Eyes down for the first number!"

## During the game
- **Pace:** call a number every 10–15 s with **Call next**. Say each number as digits and as a whole, for example "Seven-seven, 77", then repeat it once.
- **Catch-up for players:** every 10 numbers or so, click **Copy called numbers** and paste the list into the chat. It also settles "did you call X?" questions.
- **Banter:** read the nickname and add a joke. For example: "Two little ducks, 22!"
- **Claim check:**
  1. Pause auto-call.
  2. Pick the prize in the console.
  3. Type the numbers the player reads out.
  4. The console says Valid or Not valid.
  5. If it's valid, click **Record** and announce the winner.
- **Wrong claim:** "Oops, bogey! Not all called yet. Everyone carry on."
- **Prize order:** announce the order up front so nobody is confused.
  - Suggested order: Early Five → Lucky Seven → Top Line → Middle Line → Bottom Line → Four Corners → Star → Full House → Second Full House.
  - Several prizes can be open at once, so check each claim against its own prize.
- **If tech fails:**
  - If the page glitches, refresh. The game is saved and resumes.
  - **Undo** removes the last call if you misclicked.
  - If a player says they can't hear you, paste the called-numbers list into the chat.

## Closing (about 2 min, about 12:50–1:00)

>
> Thank you for being part of **GIVE 2026**. Prize details will follow by email. Have a great rest of your day!"
