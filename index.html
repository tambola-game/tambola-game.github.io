    Walk-in tickets

    Tambola rules used on every ticket — open to verify
    1. A ticket is a grid of 3 rows × 9 columns (27 cells).
    2. It holds exactly 15 numbers; the other 12 cells are blank.
    3. Every row has exactly 5 numbers and 4 blanks.
    4. Each column has its own number range (see the example ticket): column 1 = 1–9, column 2 = 10–19 … column 8 = 70–79, column 9 = 80–90.
    5. Every column has at least 1 and at most 3 numbers, so no column is empty.
    6. Inside a column, numbers increase from top to bottom.
    7. No number repeats on a ticket; all numbers are whole numbers from 1 to 90.

    Extra guarantees from this app

    • No two tickets ever have the same 15 numbers, across Excel, walk-in and earlier sessions.
    • No two tickets share an identical row (same 5 numbers), so two players cannot complete the same line on the same call.
    • Every ticket is checked against the rules above before it is shown or saved; an invalid ticket is never issued.
    • Numbers come from the browser's secure random generator (or a seeded generator if you switch on the seed option).
