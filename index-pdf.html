    Walk-in tickets

    Tambola rules used on every ticket — open to verify
    1. A ticket is a grid of 3 rows × 9 columns (27 cells) holding exactly 15 numbers; the other 12 cells are blank.
    2. Every row has exactly 5 numbers and 4 blanks.
    3. Column ranges: 1–9, 10–19, 20–29, 30–39, 40–49, 50–59, 60–69, 70–79, 80–90.
    4. Every column has at least 1 and at most 3 numbers, in increasing order from top to bottom.
    5. No number repeats on a ticket; all numbers are whole numbers from 1 to 90.
    6. No two tickets share the same 15 numbers or an identical row, across every page and session that uses this registry.
